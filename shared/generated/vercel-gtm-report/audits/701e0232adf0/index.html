Methodology

Our structured 3-step approach to evaluating the user experience

1

Scan

Appium launches the Android app and performs bounded safe navigation across in-app screens.

2

Extract

The pipeline captures screenshots, XML hierarchies, screen semantics, tappables, and interaction outcomes.

3

Analyze

Deterministic mobile checks evaluate touch targets, content descriptions, onboarding length, action availability, and navigation evidence before optional VLM/LLM synthesis.

4

Report

The shared seven-axis GTM report translates mobile evidence into prioritized UX/UI recommendations.

Excessive onboarding flow creates commercial conversion barrier

Issue: On Onboarding Flow, the review found that 'Excessive onboarding flow creates commercial conversion barrier' may not be fully supported. Users must answer many personal questions before seeing any functional feature, which is a friction point for mobile users expecting quick value.

Why it matters: This matters because people struggle to understand where they are, what comes next, or how the experience is organized. In a GTM context, visible friction in Onboarding Flow can reduce clarity, trust, or conversion readiness.

Direction: Implement progressive profiling: ask only the most critical questions up front, then allow users to explore the workout plan and return later to complete optional details.

Why it matters: This matters because prospects may not immediately understand why this product is relevant for their context or what step to take next. In a GTM context, visible friction in Onboarding Flow can reduce clarity, trust, or conversion readiness.

Issue: On Onboarding Screens, the review found that 'Repetitive motivational messaging reduces impact' may not be fully supported. Screens 12 and 14 both use generic motivational copy such as "Let's move now!" and "Count on us!" without referencing the user's specific goals.

Why it matters: Repeated generic copy becomes background noise, diminishing the perceived personalization and making key messages less memorable.

Direction: Tailor motivational copy to the user's selected goals (e.g., "Great, you want to lose weight – here's how we’ll help you hit 8 lb in 30 days!") and vary phrasing across steps.
