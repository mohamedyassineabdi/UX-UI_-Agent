Priority Issues

Only the pain points that matter most

01
5.2
!Major Issue

Website layout breaks on phone screens

Issue: On Home, the audit found that the website does not adapt reliably from desktop to phone. Desktop viewport 1440x900; phone viewport 390x844. The phone render exposes desktop-layout content that does not adapt correctly (measured overflow: 0px).

Why it matters: This matters because mobile visitors cannot evaluate offers, navigation, or promotions with confidence when the page keeps desktop layout assumptions on a phone. In a GTM context, this can directly reduce discovery, store/product engagement, and trust for first-time mobile users.

Direction: Rebuild the responsive breakpoint for the homepage and product/category templates: remove fixed-width rows, let promotional/product grids collapse to one column, make hero/media blocks fluid, and test at 390px, 430px, 768px, and desktop widths before publishing.

Trust & Accessibility

Commercial impact: Visible trust and accessibility gaps increase perceived risk and narrow the reachable audience.

Lead issue: Each page title exactly matches the wording of the related navigation menu link.

Observed friction: On Home, the audit found that 'Each page title exactly matches the wording of the related navigation menu link.' is not fully met. 0/5 page titles matched navigation labels after normalization that removes site-brand suffixes and title framing. Recommended move: Add or strengthen reassurance cues, improve visible legibility and control clarity, and remove avoidable trust-damaging signals.
